Paste each into row 2 of its column (only if the column is blank on new rows), press Enter.

ChecklistDone (AD2):
=(LEN([@ChecklistSummary])-LEN(SUBSTITUTE([@ChecklistSummary],"""isChecked"":true","")))/LEN("""isChecked"":true")

ChecklistOpen (AE2):
=[@ChecklistItemCount]-[@ChecklistDone]

IsOverdue (AF2):
=IF(OR([@DueDateTime]="",[@PercentComplete]=100),"No",IF(DATEVALUE(LEFT([@DueDateTime],10))<TODAY(),"Yes","No"))

DaysOverdue (AG2):
=IF([@IsOverdue]="Yes",TODAY()-DATEVALUE(LEFT([@DueDateTime],10)),0)

DaysToDue (AH2):
=IF(OR([@DueDateTime]="",[@PercentComplete]=100),"",DATEVALUE(LEFT([@DueDateTime],10))-TODAY())
